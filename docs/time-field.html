Time field

Collects hour, minute, and period in separate, keyboard-friendly segments. An optional hidden value keeps the submitted time in a consistent 24-hour format.

Default

Use the arrow keys on a focused segment to step its value.

Reminder time
:

Enter digits or use Up and Down to step the focused segment.

09:30 AM
