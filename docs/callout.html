Callout

A callout for important messages. Supports default and destructive variants with optional icons and actions.

Default

Informational callout with icon, title, and description.

Account updated successfully

Your profile information has been saved. Changes will be reflected immediately.

<div class="callout" role="alert">
  <svg class="callout-icon" aria-hidden="true">...</svg>
  <div class="callout-content">
    <h5 class="callout-title">Account updated</h5>
    <p class="callout-description">Your profile has been saved.</p>
  </div>
</div>

Destructive

Error or warning variant.

Payment failed

Your payment could not be processed. Please check your payment method and try again.

<div class="callout" data-variant="destructive" role="alert">
  <svg class="callout-icon" aria-hidden="true">...</svg>
  <div class="callout-content">
    <h5 class="callout-title">Payment failed</h5>
    <p class="callout-description">Please try again.</p>
  </div>
</div>

With action

Callout with an action button. Keep the action as a native, keyboard-focusable button.

Dark mode is now available

Enable it under your profile settings to get started.

<div class="callout" role="alert">
  <svg class="callout-icon" aria-hidden="true">...</svg>
  <div class="callout-content">
    <h5 class="callout-title">New feature</h5>
    <p class="callout-description">Enable it in settings.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="callout-action">
    <button class="btn" type="button" data-variant="outline" data-size="sm">Enable</button>
  </div>
</div>
