Context menu

Keeps secondary actions close to a specific object. Right-click the example or focus it and press Shift+F10; important actions should also remain discoverable through a visible control or another route.

File actions

The menu opens at the pointer or beside the focused trigger and keeps focus inside its action list until dismissal.

quarterly-report.csv Right-click or press Shift+F10 for actions.
