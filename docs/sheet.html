Sheet

Presents a modal task against one edge of the viewport. It suits supporting work that benefits from preserving some visual connection to the page behind it. The sheet opens from its chosen edge with a short transition.

Right

Default side. The sheet slides quickly from the right edge—commonly used for edit-profile or settings panels.

<!-- Trigger -->
<button type="button" class="btn" data-variant="secondary"
        data-sheet-trigger="sheet-right"
        aria-haspopup="dialog">Open Right Sheet</button>
